Welcome to Flavor

Quakuk's Buffet brings you an all-you-can-eat experience with both local Ghanaian and continental dishes. From pork with fried yams and palava sauce with ampesi to seafood macaroni and cheese, plus refreshing mocktails and espresso martinis. Reserve a table and come hungry.
